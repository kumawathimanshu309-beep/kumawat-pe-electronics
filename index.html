Our Story

My chacha was an electrician in Sanganer. Growing up, I watched him fix things other people had given up on — with patience, not panic. When I came to Vaishali Nagar in 2013, I started with just a toolbox and my two-wheeler. The first year was slow. But one afternoon I got a call from an aunty on C-Block whose kitchen pipe had burst the night before and the previous plumber hadn't returned her call. I reached in 45 minutes. She's still a regular customer today — and two of her daughters have called me for their new flats.

That's the whole business model, really. Show up. Show up on time. Don't lie about what's wrong. Don't charge for things that aren't needed. In twelve years I've never had to advertise — almost every client comes from someone who called me before.

I work with one trained assistant, Deepak, who's been with me since 2019. We do not subcontract. If you book Sharma P&E, it's us who comes.
